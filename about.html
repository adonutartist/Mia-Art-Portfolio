ABOUT ME!

Hello! My name is Miami/Mia and I'm a 2D Artist and Animator.

I've been drawing digitally for 8 years and I'm looking forward to keep continuing my art journey.

If you enjoy my content, you can drop a follow on any of my socials or check out my site :D

MY ART JOURNEY

Like any other kid, I was drawing and painting things during art class or when it was required for a school project. Obviously, my art wasn't any special.

Everything changed when I stumbled across videos from the art community, such as animation memes, story time, etc. I became really interested and wanted to learn how to do that as well, so I could make my own Youtube channel and become successful.

All I had was my phone in order to make digital art and that's where it al started. Occasionally, I would draw traditional art. My notebooks in middle school are full of doodles lol.

My art was really influenced by other artists at first, like Crystal Wish, Corina the Wolf, and at some point Qwerts, till I found my own art style. It has changed over the years, and I'm still working on it.

It is more of a self learning journey. I didn't follow many tutorials online, but I'm proud of the growth I have and I'll keep working hard on the art I make.

I hope one day I'll be able to inspire someone just like other artists inspired me when I was a kid <3

To my younger self, thank you for not giving up. I love you and I'm proud of you.
